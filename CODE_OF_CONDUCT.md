# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MedLink contributors commit to a respectful, inclusive and professional environment. We welcome participation regardless of background, identity, experience level, language or disability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and specific in technical discussion.
- Focus feedback on code, design decisions and outcomes rather than people.
- Protect privacy: never share personal, medical, financial or credential data.
- Give credit for contributions and respect licences and authorship.
- Help maintain an accessible, beginner-friendly learning environment.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information, plagiarism, or knowingly unsafe handling of sensitive data are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner or a GitHub private report. Do not include sensitive details in public issues.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or take other appropriate action to keep the project safe and respectful.
